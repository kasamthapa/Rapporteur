docs/LEARNING.md note (append):
- React `scrollIntoView({block:"center"})` on a nested `overflow-y:auto` sticky panel scrolls just that panel, not the whole page — that's what makes the "jump to transcript line" button not disturb your place in the analysis column.
- A single `useState` "highlighted line number" plus a `Map<number, HTMLLIElement>` of refs is enough to wire click-to-scroll-and-highlight without any extra library — toggle the same state value to clear it on a second click.
